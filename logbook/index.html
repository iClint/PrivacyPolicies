LogBook Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

LogBook is an independent iOS app for keeping a car logbook and car expense records in Australia, including for delivery and rideshare work. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your information.

In one sentence: LogBook has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Your records are stored on your phone and, if iCloud is on, in your own private iCloud, where only you can reach them. We never receive any of it.

Who we are

LogBook is built and maintained by an individual developer ("we", "us"). The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Australian Taxation Office, DoorDash, Uber, Menulog, Amazon Flex, or any delivery or rideshare platform. Those names, where they appear, refer only to the services a driver works for.

Location

With your permission, LogBook uses your location only while a trip is recording, including in the background, to measure how far you drive and when the car stopped moving. It is used to suggest your finishing odometer and finish time.

  • The app keeps the distance and driving time, not a map of your route.
  • For a personal trip, the start and finish points are turned into a suburb name using Apple's address lookup. That lookup sends the location to Apple to answer; Apple's privacy policy covers it.
  • Location stops when you stop the trip. You can turn off GPS distance in the app's Settings, or deny location in iOS Settings, and enter odometer readings yourself.

Camera and photos

With your permission, LogBook uses the camera or your photo library so you can attach a receipt to an expense. When Read fuel receipts is on, the app reads the receipt's printed figures using Apple's on-device text recognition — the image is processed entirely on your phone and is never uploaded. A receipt is only kept if you save it with the expense.

Notifications and Siri

The "Still on a trip?" reminder is a local notification created on your phone; nothing is sent to us. If you use the Siri shortcut to start a business trip, your voice request is handled by Siri under Apple's privacy policy.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not operate any server, and the app never sends your records to us.
  • We do not require an account, name, email or any sign-in.
  • We do not collect analytics or telemetry about you or your usage.
  • We do not include advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information.

Where your data is sent

Only to Apple, and only for the features above: your private iCloud (sync and backups, if iCloud is on), Apple's address lookup (suburb names for personal trips) and Siri (if you use it). Exports you make — the logbook and expenses spreadsheets and receipts — go only where you choose to share them.

Deleting your data

You can delete trips, expenses and cars in the app; they stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days so you can undo, then are removed for good. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the phone. To remove the copy in your iCloud, go to iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → LogBook and delete its data. We hold nothing anywhere else.

Children

LogBook is a tool for working drivers and is not directed at children under 13.
